# data/: precomputed FHLS data

Every file here is generated by `engine/` from NASA data, and every file is real (`"synthetic": false`). The formats are defined in `DATA_CONTRACT.md`.

| File | What it is | Made by | From |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ephemeris.json` / `.bin` | Sun and Earth positions relative to the Moon's centre, hourly for 2027–2028 (17,544 steps) | `engine/make_ephemeris.py` | JPL NAIF SPICE: DE440 ephemeris and lunar orientation |
| `horizon/south_2km/` | Terrain skyline (180 directions) every 2 km, south of about 84°S | `engine/make_horizon.py` | LOLA LDEM_80S_40M and LDEM_75S_240M |
| `horizon/fine_*/` | The same every 250 m around the Artemis candidate sites | `engine/make_horizon.py` | the same |
| `horizon/north_2km/` | The same every 2 km, north of about 84°N | `engine/make_horizon.py north_2km` | LOLA LDEM_80N_40M and LDEM_75N_240M |
| `horizon/global_fine/` | Skyline (72 directions) every 0.25° (about 7.5 km) across the whole Moon, low precision, one tile of about 230 KB loaded per click | `engine/make_horizon_global.py fine` | LOLA LDEM_64 (474 m) |
| `horizon/global_coarse.bin` | Skyline (72 directions) every 1° across the whole Moon, low precision | `engine/make_horizon_global.py` | LOLA LDEM_16 |
| `terrain/` | 16-bit PNG height tiles of the south polar region (`index.json`) and north polar region (`index_north.json`) for the 3D viewer | `engine/make_terrain.py [south\|north]` | LOLA LDEM_80S_40M / LDEM_80N_40M |
| `terrain/detail/`, `terrain/index_detail.json` | Full-resolution 40 m tiles (lod 2) around the 7 Artemis sites | `engine/make_terrain_detail.py artemis` | LOLA LDEM_80S_40M |
| `terrain/global/`, `terrain/index_global.json` | Whole-Moon 3D terrain at 474 m: 990 tiles of 8° × 8°, heights in 2 m steps, seams exact | `engine/make_terrain_detail.py global` | LOLA LDEM_64 |
| `sites.json`, `sites_meta.json` | Named places and Artemis region pins, each with its source | `engine/sites.py` | NASA Artemis III update (2024), IAU Gazetteer, published studies |

How accurate this is: see `engine/ACCURACY.md`. It was checked against NASA's LOLA illumination map: 0.8 percentage points mean difference, correlation 0.99.
